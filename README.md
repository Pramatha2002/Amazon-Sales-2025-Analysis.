# Amazon Sales 2025 Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Amazon sales data for 2025 using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance, product performance, order status, and customer locations to help identify trends and business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products by revenue and quantity sold.
- Understand order status, including completed and canceled orders.
- Compare sales performance across product categories and customer locations.
- Present key metrics through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Tracks revenue across months.
- **Product Analysis:** Identifies the top 5 products by revenue and quantity sold.
- **Category Analysis:** Compares revenue across product categories.
- **Order Status Analysis:** Shows the distribution of order statuses.
- **Location Analysis:** Compares sales across customer locations.
- **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to explore the data using slicers.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Refrigerators generated the highest revenue among the products shown in the dashboard.
- Smartwatches had the highest quantity sold among the products shown.
- Electronics and Home Appliances contributed approximately 96% of total revenue.
- The dashboard helps identify sales trends and areas that may require further investigation.

Created an interactive Excel dashboard to summarize sales performance and support data-driven business decisions.
